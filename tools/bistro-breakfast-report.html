11 / Bistro operations

A clearer start
to breakfast.

Turn Stay's Coupon Activity CSV into a daily guest list and coupon totals for staffing. Review multiday coupons, then print or save your report as a PDF.

1. Upload your report

Export with Status: All. Upload one report at a time; a new upload replaces the previous report.

Your guest data stays in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or saved to the website. Closing or refreshing clears it.

Ready for your CSV.

2. Review breakfast dates

Stay lists unused coupons over a whole date range. It does not say which morning each coupon belongs to. Assign these records to breakfast dates before using the totals for staffing.

Each CSV row counts as one coupon record, even when rows look identical. Only BKFST 1 / 1B is included. Food-and-beverage credits and alcohol coupons are listed separately below. Room numbers are not in this export.
Quick assignment for future-use stays

Use only after confirming that the range represents arrival and departure dates and coupons are issued evenly each morning. This applies only to fully unassigned Future Use groups whose counts divide evenly across the selected mornings. Other groups stay in review.

Excluded coupon types

3. Choose dates & create your PDF

In the print window, choose “Save as PDF.” The PDF includes a staffing overview, daily guest lists, and any unresolved coupons. Counts are coupon entitlements, not guaranteed diners or a full breakfast forecast.
